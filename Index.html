All about me and what I'm passionate about

I'm deeply passionate about helping people, and if there's an opportunity to make a difference, I'll do everything I can to help!

One of my proudest accomplishments was helping establish wheelchair-accessible eye exams as the new standard across Walmart Vision Centres in Canada. Working collaboratively with our Store Support team and Health Innovation, I helped develop a return-on-investment proposal that demonstrated the value and impact of this initiative. My store was the first to receive the new equipment, and today, all stores requiring replacement examination chairs automatically receive wheelchair-accessible equipment.

By having this equipment, Walmart can help make eye care more affordable and accessible to more people. Most importantly, this initiative helps remove barriers to essential eye care, ensuring that more people, including those who use wheelchairs, can access the services they need.

During my time managing a Vision Centre, I took on responsibilities that extended well beyond the optical department. I made a point of learning about pharmacy operations and the services offered by both departments so I could better understand how they complemented one another and identify opportunities to improve the overall customer experience.

I was always looking for ways to promote the business and bring more visibility to the services we offered. From creating social media posts highlighting promotions and services to finding opportunities for the optical and pharmacy departments to work together, I enjoyed thinking creatively about how to grow the business and better serve our customers.

As a Market Champion, I became a go-to resource for other locations, helping resolve complex issues and supporting stores that needed help improving their performance. I took pride in identifying problems, finding practical solutions, and helping teams get back on track.

I also facilitated training for both optical and pharmacy staff, helping team members develop the knowledge and skills needed to provide excellent care and service to customers and patients.

Although I'm now working toward a new career in web development, that same problem-solving mindset and willingness to help others have followed me. I'm still someone people turn to for support, whether it's working through a challenge, sharing knowledge, or finding a better way to do something.

The industry may be changing, but the mindset remains the same: keep learning, solve problems, support the team, and always look for ways to improve.

When I’m not working in the optical world, you’ll usually find me tackling a home improvement project — laying laminate flooring, tiling kitchens, building custom bookshelves, or installing pocket doors.

I’ve always enjoyed learning new things and challenging myself. I’m not one to shy away from a project just because I’ve never done it before. If anything, that’s what makes it exciting. I enjoy figuring things out, solving problems, and turning ideas into something tangible.

Whether I’m working with eyewear, writing code, or building something with my own two hands, I’m driven by the same things: curiosity, creativity, and the satisfaction of learning something new.
